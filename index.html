How each figure was checked

Verified means the amount comes from an official record. The LCUP balance is taken from the student account statement. The AMA admission fee of ₱1,000 is stated on the official OEd admissions page for bachelor degree programs. The PSA Birth Certificate price is the published 2026 rate, ₱365 delivered or ₱155 walk in at a PSA outlet.

Estimate means no fixed amount is published. LCUP lists no prices for registrar documents, so a ₱1,500 allowance is used, covering the transcript, good moral, honorable dismissal, and certified course descriptions of all subjects taken. The photos and small items line is a working allowance. AMA computes tuition per subject after the credit evaluation, so the per unit, per term, downpayment, and until graduation figures are projections built from current published rates and the evaluated curriculum. The final bill appears on the student dashboard before any payment is made.
